FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
• Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
  • Working Production Prototype & Live Deployment: Fully operational software running live at https://quantagreen.onrender.com/, tested across 7 major shipping routes (JNPT, Kochi, Colombo, Chennai, Vizag, Kolkata, Dubai, Singapore).
  • Direct Maritime Regulatory Alignment: Follows IMO MEPC.337(76) MARPOL Annex VI formulas directly, making outputs immediately usable for fleet ESG and compliance filings.
  • Commercial Port Infrastructure Integration: Tracks cold-ironing shore power availability at major Indian port terminals to eliminate berthed auxiliary generator emissions. (Sister urban fleet system: https://quantafleet.onrender.com/)

• Potential challenges and risks

  • Alternative Fuel Price Volatility: Green methanol and ammonia prices fluctuate based on renewable energy availability.
  • Severe Monsoon Sea States: Rough swell (Beaufort 6–8) sharply increases hydrodynamic drag, threatening schedule deadlines.
  • Port Turnaround Delays: Berth congestion can force auxiliary generator idling if shore power cables are unavailable.

• Strategies for overcoming these challenges

  • Dynamic Carbon-Price Parity Solver: The QIO automatically calculates break-even carbon tax levels where green fuels become cheaper than fossil MGO.
  • Weather-Aware Speed Optimization: Slow-steaming during calm sectors buffers schedule reserves for storm-affected sectors.
  • Cold-Ironing Priority Scheduling: Recommends optimal berth allocation to ensure high-emissions vessels secure shore-power berths.
