IDEA TITLE: QUANTAFLEET
Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
• Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
  • QuantaFleet is a full-stack, quantum-inspired intelligent route optimization platform designed to solve the NP-hard Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP) in large-scale urban transportation networks.
  • Ingests real OpenStreetMap (OSMNx) street topology (6,552 road intersections across Mumbai) and dynamically overlays simulated real-time traffic speeds and congestion factors on graph edges.
  • Formulates multi-vehicle routing as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) Hamiltonian and executes a simulated quantum annealing metaheuristic with quantum tunneling to generate global near-optimal vehicle paths.

• Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

  • Quantum Tunneling Metaheuristic: Simulates transverse-field quantum tunneling (Ptunnel = exp(−√(2mΔE) / ℏΓ)) allowing the solver to escape deep local minima where classical greedy/GA algorithms get stuck.
  • Sub-Second Optimization Speed: Optimizes 80-node delivery instances in < 450 ms with real-time dynamic re-routing for unexpected bottlenecks.
  • NISQ & Cloud Ready: Deployed live on cloud today, with direct dispatch compatibility to AWS Braket and D-Wave QPUs.

• Methodology and process for implementation (Workflow & Prototype Architecture)

  • Step 1: Graph Network Modeling — Ingests real road geometry from OpenStreetMap for Mumbai. Nodes represent intersections; edges hold lengths, speed limits, and lanes.
  • Step 2: Traffic Edge Weighting — Applies synthetic and live congestion multipliers (0.5× free flow to 3.0× rush hour) to compute edge travel times: Weight = Travel_Time (seconds).
  • Step 3: Distance & Time Cost Matrix — Computes all-pairs Dijkstra shortest paths over the traffic-weighted road network.
  • Step 4: QUBO Hamiltonian Formulation — Formulates multi-vehicle constraints (capacity limits, single-visit requirement, depot return) and distance objective:
    H = A·Hvisit + B·Hcapacity + C·Σ Cij xi,t xj,t+1
  • Step 5: Quantum-Inspired Annealing — Runs simulated annealing with transverse-field tunneling to find the global optimum.
  • Step 6: Dashboard & Dynamic ETA — Renders route geometry, cumulative arrival ETAs per stop, and live traffic heatmap on UI.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
• Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
  • Technically Proven & Live: A functioning end-to-end prototype is already implemented and running live at https://quantafleet.onrender.com/ with 6,552 real Mumbai road network nodes, delivering validated route solutions in under 450 ms.
  • Zero Cost Barrier to Entry: Built on open-source frameworks (FastAPI, React, OSMNx, NetworkX). Runs on standard cloud virtual machines without requiring expensive physical cryogenic quantum hardware today.
  • Seamless Enterprise Integration: Clean REST APIs allow turnkey integration into existing TMS (Transportation Management Systems), ERPs (SAP/Oracle), and driver mobile applications.

• Potential challenges and risks

  • Graph Scale Complexity: Large metropolitan graphs (e.g. 50,000+ street segments) can increase shortest-path computation overhead.
  • Dynamic Rush-Hour Fluctuations: Sudden accidents or road blockages can invalidate pre-planned routes midway through delivery.
  • GPS Telemetry Latency: Inconsistent cellular connectivity in dense urban corridors causing delayed vehicle tracking.

• Strategies for overcoming these challenges

  • Graph Contraction & Hierarchical Routing: Uses Contraction Hierarchies and KD-Tree spatial indexing to perform path lookups in < 5 ms.
  • Partial Dynamic Re-Routing: Built-in /api/optimize/reroute re-optimizes remaining unfinished legs dynamically without altering completed deliveries.
  • Asynchronous State & Edge Caching: In-memory route caching and WebSocket event channels decouple optimization from intermittent telemetry.
