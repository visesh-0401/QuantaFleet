IDEA TITLE

Proposed solution

QuantaFleet is a traffic-aware route-planning platform that models the road network as a weighted graph and uses Quantum Particle Swarm Optimization to assign and sequence delivery stops.

How it addresses the problem

  • Updates route costs from simulated or live traffic inputs.
  • Optimizes time, distance, congestion exposure and vehicle capacity together.
  • Replans affected routes when traffic conditions change.

Innovation and uniqueness

  • QPSO maintains a probabilistic search distribution for stronger global exploration.
  • A congestion-aware objective converts traffic conditions into route penalties.
  • The platform compares QPSO with classical heuristics and exact baselines on the same scenario.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Methodology and implementation process

1. Network ingestion
Build a weighted graph from road data, delivery nodes and vehicle constraints.
2. Dynamic cost estimation
Update edge travel time with simulated traffic, congestion and road-status signals.
3. QPSO route search
Encode route assignments and stop order, then minimize a penalized VRP objective.
4. Constraint repair and validation
Enforce capacity, depot, service and route-feasibility rules.
5. Benchmark and visualize
Compare QPSO against greedy, classical metaheuristic and exact small-instance baselines.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Feasibility of the idea

  • Runs on conventional CPUs, so a quantum computer is not required for the MVP.
  • Uses open road-network data and reproducible simulated traffic scenarios during development.
  • Uses a modular API so traffic feeds, solvers and visualizations can evolve independently.
  • Evaluates exact methods only on small instances and uses heuristics at larger scale.

Viability for deployment

  • Suitable for urban delivery fleets, municipal mobility control rooms and logistics operators.
  • Supports staged rollout: offline planning, simulated live updates, then feed integration.
  • Exposes measurable operational outputs before any production integration.

Potential challenges, risks and mitigation

Traffic data quality and availabilityStart with calibrated simulation and retain a fallback travel-time model when a feed is stale.
Premature convergence in optimizationTune QPSO parameters, use diversified initialization and retain a repair-and-restart strategy.
Large search spacesPartition by service zone, limit candidate edges and benchmark scalability by fleet size.
Route feasibilityApply explicit capacity and service constraints, then validate every returned route before display.
